Executive Personal Assistant

Private Residence Placement · Lagos

Domestic staffingOn-sitePosted today

About this role

We are seeking an organised, proactive Executive Personal Assistant to support a private household. This role suits someone who is discreet, confident, and comfortable coordinating a busy daily schedule.

What you will do

  • Manage calendars, appointments, and household schedules.
  • Coordinate errands, travel arrangements, and correspondence.
  • Maintain confidential records and communicate with service providers.
  • Support day-to-day household administration with professionalism.

What you will bring

  • Previous experience as a personal or executive assistant.
  • Excellent organisation, communication, and time-management skills.
  • A high level of discretion and attention to detail.
  • Confidence using email, calendars, and common office tools.
